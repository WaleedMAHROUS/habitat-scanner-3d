Privacy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Habitat Scanner 3D is a screening tool for aerodrome wildlife hazard management. It is operated by Waleed Mahrous (StopBIRDSTRIKE). This page says what data the tool handles. You do not need an account to use it.

Who to contact

Questions, access, correction or deletion requests: info@waleedmahrous.com.

The tool does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not ask you to log in.

Who else receives data

  • Cloudflare: hosts the tool and runs its relay servers (map data and the feedback form).
  • Resend: delivers the feedback form to the developer's mailbox as an email (the sending region used is Tokyo).
  • Hostinger: hosts the developer's mailbox and domain.
  • GoatCounter: visit statistics, as described above.
  • Vercel and Google Earth Engine: the satellite water service receives the airport coordinates and area (no personal data).
  • Content providers loaded by your browser when you open the tool: code libraries (unpkg, jsDelivr, cdnjs), fonts (Google Fonts), the airport list (GitHub), satellite imagery and land-cover maps (Esri ArcGIS, ESA WorldCover through VITO Terrascope). As with any website, these providers see your IP address when your browser fetches the files.

Some of these providers are outside your country, including outside the EU and the UK.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Write to info@waleedmahrous.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Not for safety reporting

The feedback form is not a channel for reporting wildlife strikes or safety occurrences. Use your aerodrome's or authority's reporting system for those.
